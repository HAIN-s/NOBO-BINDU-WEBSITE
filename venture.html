Our Process

From pitch to partnership.

We keep our process transparent, founder-friendly, and efficient — because we respect your time as much as your idea.

Step 01

Initial Application

Submit a brief overview of your idea, team, and traction. We review every application with care and respond within 2 weeks.

Step 02

Introductory Call

We get to know you — your vision, your story, the problem you're solving, and why you're the right person to solve it.

Step 03

Deep Dive & Due Diligence

We dig deeper into your market, business model, and team dynamics to ensure alignment before moving forward.

Step 04

Term Sheet & Partnership

We agree on terms, sign the partnership, and officially welcome you into the Nobo Bindu family — ready to grow together.
